# Hendi Saputra Portfolio Design Direction

## Identity

An editorial developer portfolio that presents Hendi as a full-stack product builder. The site should feel deliberate, technically credible, and personal without using a portrait in the hero.

## Audience

Hiring managers, engineering leaders, product teams, and potential collaborators evaluating real shipped work.

## Personality

Restrained, confident, precise, and quietly cinematic. Avoid corporate polish, startup-template language, and decorative technology clichés.

## Visual language

- Near-black canvas with warm sand as the single accent.
- Cabinet Grotesk for display type, Switzer for body copy, and IBM Plex Mono for small technical labels.
- Editorial composition with strong type hierarchy, structural whitespace, fine rules, and varied section rhythm.
- The Uiverse "Tasty Dragon 12" starfield is an intentional identity motif in the hero and selected transitions. It must remain subtle and must not become a generic space theme.
- Real project screenshots are the primary visual evidence. Do not replace them with mock dashboards, fake terminals, generated product shots, or generic illustrations.

## Interaction

- Motion guides attention and reveals structure. It must never delay reading or compete with the work.
- Preserve Motion, Lenis, scroll progress, accordion project details, and reduced-motion support.
- All controls need visible keyboard focus, touch feedback, and real behavior.

## Content

- Use only real projects, roles, links, screenshots, dates, and claims.
- Keep the hero text-led. Do not add a hero portrait.
- Keep project writing concrete and concise. Avoid fake metrics, generic testimonials, inflated AI language, and template sections.

## Dials

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2

## Decision reasons

- Color: near-black keeps project imagery and typography dominant; sand adds warmth and continuity without becoming decoration.
- Layout: varied editorial compositions reflect the range of work and prevent a repetitive card-grid portfolio.
- Typography: the display, body, and mono roles separate narrative, explanation, and technical metadata.
- Spacing: generous desktop whitespace creates hierarchy; mobile spacing compresses to keep the reading rhythm practical.
- Starfield: it is a previously approved personal motif, used only as atmospheric depth behind content.
- Dark theme: it is the established portfolio identity and supports the restrained cinematic direction, so no theme toggle is required.

## Do not introduce

Gradients as a brand substitute, glassmorphism, blobs, bento layouts, generic card grids, fake metrics, fake testimonials, decorative AI icons, excessive pills, or a visual clone of another portfolio.
